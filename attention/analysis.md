# Analysis

## Layer 3, Head 1
Tokens appear to attend strongly to the token that immediately precedes them
in the sequence, suggesting this head captures local positional relationships.

Example Sentences:
- "The dog barked loudly at the stranger."
- "The chef prepared a delicious meal for the guests."

## Layer 5, Head 4
Pronouns appear to attend strongly to related pronouns and nouns nearby,
suggesting this head captures coreference relationships. For example, "she" 
and "her" attend to each other, indicating the model links pronouns to their 
referents.

Example Sentences:
- "She did her homework before dinner."
- "The dog barked loudly at the stranger."
